Good to know

Questions we hear often

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. Jackets are welcome but never required.

Can you cater for allergies?

Yes. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will adapt every course.

Do you welcome children?

Children over 10 are welcome in the dining room; we offer a smaller menu on request.

Is there parking?

Valet parking is available from 5 pm for $18, and the Larkspur garage is two minutes away.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please let us know 24 hours ahead. Chef’s counter bookings carry a $50 per person deposit.

Can I bring my own wine?

Yes, up to two bottles per table with a $40 corkage fee, waived for every bottle bought from our list.

A table is waiting.
