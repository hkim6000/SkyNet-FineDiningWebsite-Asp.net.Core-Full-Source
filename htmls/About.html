Cooking with the seasons

Chef Elena Marchetti opened Aurelle in an old print shop on Larkspur Street. The menu was three pages long and changed every week; it still does.

Most of what we cook comes from twelve farms and fishers within fifty miles. Our kitchen garden grows the herbs and flowers, our pastry chef bakes the bread every morning, and our sommelier looks after a cellar of 600 wines.

What we believe

Four simple promises

Season first

The menu changes when the market does, not the other way round.

Close to home

Twelve farms and fishers within a short drive supply most of what we cook.

Unhurried

One seating per table. Stay as long as you like.

Hospitality

Allergies, celebrations, a quiet table — tell us and we’ll take care of it.
